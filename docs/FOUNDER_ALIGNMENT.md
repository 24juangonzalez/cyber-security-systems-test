# Founder alignment checklist

This document is a conversation guide for the founders. It is not a substitute
for formation documents, invention/IP assignments, equity purchase agreements,
vesting terms, or legal and tax advice.

For each topic, write the agreed answer, the date, and any follow-up. Move major
decisions into signed documents with qualified counsel.

## Motivation and definition of success

- Why does each of us want to build this company?
- Are we aiming for a venture-scale company, a profitable small business, an
  acquisition, or are we still testing?
- What personal outcomes would count as success in 1, 3, and 10 years?
- What ethical lines or customer uses will we not cross?

## Commitment

- How many hours per week will each founder commit now?
- When, if ever, will each founder go full-time?
- What income, family, school, health, or immigration constraints matter?
- How long can each founder operate without salary?
- What happens if one founder consistently cannot meet the commitment?

- Which decisions require unanimous agreement?
- What happens when we reach a deadlock?
- Which commitments can either founder make without the other founder's
  approval, and up to what dollar amount?

## Equity, money, and vesting

- What equity split do we propose, and what contributions justify it?
- Will founder equity vest? What are the schedule, cliff, start date, and rules
  if someone leaves?
- Who has already contributed cash, equipment, code, relationships, or other
  assets, and how are those contributions treated?
- When can founders be paid or reimbursed?
- How much dilution are we comfortable accepting, and for what milestones?

Do not rely on a handshake for these items. Tax elections can have strict
deadlines; obtain jurisdiction-specific legal and tax advice promptly.

## Intellectual property and confidentiality

- What pre-existing IP does each founder bring, if any?
- Has every founder, employee, and contractor assigned relevant work product to
  the company in writing?
- Do employment, school, grant, or contractor agreements create conflicts?
- What is open source, what is proprietary, and who approves new dependencies?
- Where will confidential company and customer information be stored?

## Working relationship

- How do we give candid feedback?
- How quickly do we acknowledge messages and urgent incidents?
- What meeting cadence and written updates will we use?
- How will we address missed commitments or declining trust?
- How will we prevent a friendship disagreement from becoming a company
  stalemate—and vice versa?

## Departure and difficult scenarios

- What happens to unvested and vested equity if a founder leaves?
- Can a departing founder keep using company IP or compete with the company?
- What happens after death, disability, divorce, bankruptcy, or misconduct?
- Can shares be sold or transferred? Does the company have a right to buy them?
- What process applies if one founder wants to sell the company and the other
  does not?
- How will disputes be mediated or resolved?
