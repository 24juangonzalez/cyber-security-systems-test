# Cybersecurity Company Strategy and Business Plan

Prepared for Juan C Gonzalez and Shawn Sebastian Punch

Research date 10 October 2026 | Version 0.1 | Founder decision document

This plan proposes a company focused on helping organizations verify security decisions through traceable evidence. It compares possible markets, recommends a first commercial experiment, and defines the operating and financial evidence needed before expanding. The ambition is international leadership in a defensible category; no market leadership, customer traction, production readiness, or financing is claimed today.

Company name, legal entity, capital commitments, and funding route remain open. The structure supports future investor diligence, but the commercial case is not yet investor-validated. References identify public evidence; proposals and financial illustrations are explicitly labeled. This document broadens company planning without changing the accepted prototype scope or authorizing customer-data collection.

## Executive recommendation

Build a cybersecurity company around a durable question: did a security decision produce the intended configuration outcome, and can the organization explain that conclusion from reliable evidence? The proposed mission is to make security changes understandable, reviewable, and verifiable across organizational and technical boundaries.

The recommended initial experiment is a bounded vendor-access review and closure package for an IT provider or industrial integrator serving smaller industrial customers. It would reconcile approved access with supported exports, identify discrepancies and missing evidence, preserve a human decision, and compare subsequent evidence. This is a proposed entry point, not the complete company identity or an approved product pivot.

Three foundations could support a larger business: dependable source normalization, explicit reasoning about incomplete or contradictory evidence, and repeatable verification integrated into customers' existing work. These are capabilities to earn through measured delivery. Graphs, AI, dashboards, and an export button are not defensible advantages on their own.

Public research establishes recurring review needs and considerable competition. CIS recommends periodic authorization reviews [S03]. Industrial guidance calls for validating and maintaining architecture records [S04]. Existing products already support access reviews, configuration validation, and evidence collection [S08–S16]. The open question is whether a specific customer can complete a valuable workflow with materially less total effort using our approach.

For the next six months, use a 200-hour planning baseline across both founders, including a 30-hour reserve. Deliver one rigorously tested workflow, a competitor/manual benchmark, a defined data-handling process, and a documented commercial decision. Do not promise a production platform, a large set of integrations, or a revenue target before the evidence supports them.

The investment thesis is conditional: if customer-assisted delivery repeatedly reveals the same data and review problem, automate that repeatable core and distribute through qualified partners. If each engagement requires extensive bespoke interpretation or existing tools solve it adequately, narrow further or stop that product experiment while retaining reusable technical work.

## Company identity and ambition

Proposed mission: help organizations make and verify consequential security decisions using evidence they can inspect and control. Proposed vision: become a trusted international provider of security verification capabilities embedded in operational workflows. This is an aspiration, not a statement of present scale or market position.

Juan C Gonzalez and Shawn Sebastian Punch are the founders identified by Juan. Both contribute to product and research. Juan performs most engineering; Shawn contributes business planning and is the proposed discovery and sales lead. Both hold full-time jobs and report 4–10 project hours each weekly. These working arrangements do not assign executive titles, legal authority, equity, or security-review credentials. Biography materials should describe demonstrable work and actual experience once supplied; no industrial expertise or prior exits are inferred.

Proposed company principles are evidence before conclusions, customer control of sensitive information, explicit limits, human ownership of operational changes, and measured usefulness. Product claims must distinguish a configured relationship from usable access, a removed relationship from complete revocation, and a technical result from a legal or compliance opinion.

The company should own a coherent capability, rather than an unrelated collection of security apps. The proposed common core is a versioned record connecting the business intent, permitted evidence, technical finding, reviewer decision, and subsequent verification. Customer-specific adapters and reports would surround that core. The current repository is one technical experiment within this broader direction.

International leadership would require reference customers, independently reviewed correctness, reproducible delivery, support capacity, localization, channel trust, and lawful data operations. A large mission does not require launching in multiple jurisdictions at once. Begin with an English-language US commercial hypothesis, then enter another jurisdiction only when a qualified channel or customer creates a specific reason.

A concise proposed company description is: We develop cybersecurity software that helps teams reconcile security evidence, review access decisions, and verify changes. Our first research program focuses on third-party access in industrial and hybrid environments. We are developing and testing the product; we do not yet offer production assessments.

## Problems worth investigating

Access decisions lose context as accounts, contractors, approvals, and systems change. A recurring job is to reconcile what is approved with what source systems record, then establish what changed after review. The technical challenge includes identity matching, incomplete exports, temporal consistency, nested permissions, and business exceptions. CIS and documented access-review products establish that the task recurs [S03, S08, S10].

Industrial documentation can drift from installed configurations and from other records. A potential product would surface conflicts, missing provenance, and stale relationships, then produce a reviewable handover or change record. Joint OT guidance explicitly discusses completeness, consistency, expert validation, and ongoing maintenance [S04]. Solving this requires careful semantics; a merged spreadsheet alone does not establish the real system state.

Security-change assurance asks whether an intended configuration change achieved its defined result without unsupported conclusions. Existing network validation and security-validation products serve parts of this workflow [S11, S12, S16]. A new entrant would need a precise cross-system or delivery advantage, not a generic before-and-after screen.

Machine and AI-agent authority is a strategic research option. NIST's 2026 agent initiative and September feedback publication show active identity and authorization work [S06, S07]. A possible future experiment is a repeatable permission-change test suite for one agent execution environment. It would need to distinguish granted permissions, delegated authority, and runtime behavior. Established identity vendors already address this area [S15]. Emerging standards do not prove an accessible market or a reason to abandon the current foundation.

General compliance evidence collection is a crowded adjacent category [S14]. It may be an integration destination, rather than a product line. Avoid selling certification, legal compliance, breach prevention, or universal security posture from a narrow evidence review.

The common research theme is verifying a specific claim across imperfect records. This is intellectually and technically substantial. It becomes a business only when a buyer values the result enough to adopt a repeatable workflow.

These are management criteria, not statistical validation. Adoption and procurement evidence must precede a scaling decision.

Disqualifiers are inaccessible required evidence, excessive manual reconstruction, unavailable operational review, a competitor already delivering the outcome within the customer's existing setup, or an inability to preserve uncertainty without making the report useless. Stop or redesign rather than compensating with additional dashboards.

## First customer and commercial deliverable

The proposed first user is an IT provider or industrial integrator performing a bounded vendor-access review for a smaller operator. The potential buyer is the provider owner or delivery lead; the end customer's IT or operations owner approves scope and operational decisions. This is a channel hypothesis, not an existing relationship. Direct sales remain an alternative if providers do not value the workflow.

The trigger is a scheduled review, completion of maintenance, vendor replacement, or an access change. Scope the first deliverable to a declared set of accounts and one supported entitlement source plus a customer-maintained approval register. Do not initially promise effective access across arbitrary VPNs, firewalls, directories, and applications.

| Customer receives | What it establishes |
| --- | --- |
| Short review summary | Scope, observations, priority questions and material limits |
| Reconciliation table | How recorded memberships compare with documented approvals |
| Exceptions register | Expired approvals, missing owners, unmatched identities and contradictory evidence |
| Reviewer decision record | Who chose to retain, investigate or request a change and why |
| Follow-up comparison | What comparable evidence shows changed; what remains unresolved |
| Evidence appendix | Source references, timestamps, scope and method versions |

Microsoft documents group membership CSV exports with stable object IDs and member types [S09]. That makes one bounded importer technically plausible. It does not establish account activity, transitive group closure, VPN access, or application authorization. A missing row proves removal only when source scope and completeness justify that interpretation.

A vendor-published ConductorOne case describes legacy-system reviews and collection of removal evidence [S19]. It supports testing the workflow, but its financial-sector customer and selection bias prevent extrapolation to small industrial buyers.

Proposed customer acceptance is correct classification on agreed cases, an understandable report, manageable evidence-preparation effort, and a review outcome the customer can use. No minimum count of security findings should be promised.

## Product architecture and research program

The existing repository contains a modular Python prototype with synthetic ingestion, domain records, normalization checks, a bounded path rule, conservative comparison, reports, and an optional local UI. This is useful engineering groundwork. It is not an implementation of the proposed access-approval reconciliation workflow, a validated real-data importer, or a production evidence platform. The recorded validation baseline is 156 passing tests on 2 October 2026; this strategy review is not a fresh test or security audit.

Preserve the current industrial demonstration while testing the new workflow in a separately named module and contract. Reuse validation patterns, provenance, report rendering, and comparable-scope logic when semantics actually match. Do not reinterpret a membership exception as an attack path. The public CLI and existing fixture schema should remain compatible until a separately reviewed implementation specifies otherwise.

The research contract must define subject identity, approval purpose, resource/entitlement scope, owner, observation time, expiry, source completeness, and reviewer outcome. Names alone are insufficient identifiers. Duplicate or conflicting identities must become explicit exceptions. Use synthetic examples shaped like documented exports before accepting private data.

Maintain positive, negative, contradictory, incomplete, stale, changed-scope, duplicate-identity, and source-version cases. A correct unknown result is valuable; silently declaring resolution is not. Measure ingestion, reconciliation, rendering, peak memory, and the time a human spends preparing and reviewing inputs. Existing traversal limits are not a process memory guarantee.

AI may later explain verified results or assist mapping under review. It must not fabricate access, evidence, ownership, or approval. Any AI processing of customer information requires a separately approved data boundary and evaluated output behavior.

A future expansion should reuse a proven evidence contract and buyer workflow. Add a second entitlement source after demonstrating the first. Add a network or agent-authorization use case only after its semantics, customer need, and evaluation method are defined.

## Market sizing and route to customers

Do not derive a revenue opportunity by taking a small percentage of worldwide security spending. Define the buying unit first: one provider organization paying for a defined delivery workflow, or one direct operating customer paying for a bounded assessment. The same end customer cannot be counted again as a separate partner sale without a distinct contract.

Use US Census Statistics of US Businesses to establish a firm-level universe by industry and enterprise size, then remove organizations outside supported geography, workflows, and evidence formats [S17]. Manufacturing sites are not automatically separate buyers. Industry codes and size filters do not reveal vendor-access complexity or procurement readiness; those require additional evidence. No defensible niche TAM or SAM count has yet been calculated.

A bottom-up model should store eligible buying organizations, qualifying share, reachable accounts, conversion, annual spend per buyer, delivery workload, and retention. Calculate serviceable obtainable revenue as the lesser of commercially supported demand and feasible delivery capacity. Do not multiply an illustrative account count by unvalidated pricing and call the result a measured market.

For scale arithmetic only, 100 provider organizations at an assumed USD 10,000 per year would equal USD 1 million annual revenue. Neither input is measured demand or approved pricing. This is the magnitude that a future channel thesis would need to justify, not a forecast.

Initial routes are targeted provider research, industrial practitioner communities, and relevant manufacturing-support organizations. NIST's MEP directory is a starting point for ecosystem mapping, not an endorsement or a committed sales channel [S18]. No contact list or outreach is created or sent by this document.

Proposed acquisition sequence is a problem-specific example, technical review, bounded evaluation, purchasing discussion, paid delivery, and repeat use. Track qualified conversations, data feasibility, evaluation acceptance, paid conversion, delivery effort, and repeat purchase separately. Website traffic and favorable comments do not establish traction.

## Security and delivery governance

The product should start local and read-only, with no live credentials, active OT scanning, controller communication, or automatic changes. Local processing reduces some exposure but does not establish secure storage, secure deletion, or absence of leakage. Export files, downloaded reports, crash output, browser state, and machine access require controls before private data is accepted.

Before a customer evaluation, identify authorized scope, permitted fields, business purpose, source ownership, transfer method, storage location, access owner, retention period, deletion process, incident contact, and reviewer competence. Reject unnecessary secrets. Keep customer datasets separate. Confirm any derived benchmark rights explicitly; default to no reuse.

A sensitive importer or inference change needs focused security review and failure-case tests. Preserve reproducibility, version input contracts, reject unsupported formats clearly, and record evidence gaps. Follow secure-product development guidance as a design reference, not a certification claim [S20].

Proposed change control has three levels. Documentation and synthetic examples use normal peer review. Input, inference, and customer-data changes require a technical and security review. Commercial promises, spending, legal obligations, new data locations, or product-direction changes require both founders' recorded decision until a formal authority policy replaces this proposal.

Threats include malicious files, formula injection in spreadsheet exports, ambiguous identities, stale snapshots, tampered evidence, overbroad permissions, misleading reports, dependency compromise, and accidental customer-data retention. Scope-specific controls must be tested before supporting each output or input type. A hash detects differences; it does not independently certify source truth.

For global expansion, evaluate contractual obligations, privacy and transfer requirements, localization, support coverage, and partner competence for the actual destination. This plan makes no legal applicability determination and claims no SOC 2, ISO, IEC, or other certification.

## Risks and falsification criteria

| Risk | Early indicator | Response and proposed owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No meaningful differentiation | Existing tool completes the same job with comparable effort | Stop feature expansion; revise workflow; both founders |
| Input burden exceeds value | Manual mapping dominates review time | Narrow formats or stop importer; Juan |
| False assurance | Incomplete evidence appears resolved | Block release; strengthen contract and independent cases; Juan |
| No reachable buyer | Praise but no evaluator or purchasing owner | Reassess segment/channel; Shawn proposed |
| Consulting trap | Each review needs bespoke logic | Cap custom work; require repeatable pattern; both |
| Founder capacity overload | Reserve consumed before core milestone | Cut scope or move dates; both |
| Sensitive-data exposure | Uncontrolled exports or unclear storage | Pause intake and address incident/handling controls; both |
| Premature platform expansion | Multiple products without repeat usage | Return to one funded experiment; both |

The proposed technical gate is independent reproduction of the intended result with explicit failures on unsupported cases. The proposed usability gate is that reviewers identify the conclusion and major uncertainty without coaching. A proposed benchmark target is at least 30% lower median total review effort across five predefined synthetic cases versus a competent spreadsheet baseline, with no missed seeded critical exceptions. This is an experimental target, not an existing result or an accuracy claim. Disclose the small artificial sample and counterbalance task order to reduce learning effects.

A proposed commercial gate is two independent organizations willing to evaluate the same output and one identifiable purchasing route. A scaling gate is three paid deliveries across at least two customers, positive contribution including labor, and repeat demand. These thresholds organize decisions; they do not establish statistical market validation.

Public research can justify or eliminate experiments. It cannot establish this team's execution quality, a customer's data access, or willingness to pay. Failure to reach a gate should produce a written explanation and a narrower test, not a fabricated success metric.

## Path to a durable international business

The first horizon is a useful repeatable review. The second is a reusable evidence and verification workflow across a small number of sources. The third could be partner-delivered verification across related security decisions. Machine/agent authority and operational change assurance are expansion options only if they share the evidence model and demonstrate demand.

Developing a durable advantage requires accumulating correctly licensed, non-sensitive test cases; validated mappings; documented failure behavior; independent review; and partners who can deliver without founder correction. An open interchange specification or synthetic benchmark could build trust, subject to a deliberate IP decision. Existing proprietary-code policy is unchanged; no license or outside contribution policy is modified by this plan.

Measure leadership through adoption, repeat use, independently reproduced conclusions, low preparation burden, and partner delivery quality. Do not use unsupported superlatives such as world leading, unique, breach proof, or enterprise ready in company materials. A focused initial workflow can support a broad company ambition if later products share real capabilities and distribution.

Immediate founder decisions are whether to adopt the company mission, which one experiment to fund with time, who owns each workstream, what spending is affordable, and what evidence would cause a stop. The name and brand can follow a clear purpose; they should not delay testing it. The proposed strategy remains separate from the accepted industrial prototype until founders record a direction decision.
