# Company strategy package

Prepared 10 October 2026 and updated 26 October 2026 for founder review, with an investor-oriented
structure. This is a proposed company strategy; it does not replace DEC-003 or
imply an approved pivot.

**Mihari** is the working prototype name. It is not currently the legal or
company name, both of which remain open founder decisions.

The durable package contains seven documents:

- [Business plan and company strategy](COMPANY_STRATEGY.md): editable narrative
  covering mission, opportunities, competition, proposed offer, economics,
  governance, and growth.
- [Word business plan](Company_Strategy_and_Business_Plan.docx): formatted
  review copy with the four charts embedded.
- [Project plan](PROJECT_PLAN.md): proposed owners, hour budgets, work packages,
  and decision gates.
- [Market and discovery brief](MARKET_DISCOVERY.md): target segments, buyer
  roles, prospect sources, outreach guidance, interview questions, market
  boundary, and evidence milestones.
- [Founder action plan](FOUNDER_ACTION_PLAN.docx): Word review copy of the
  proposed 12-week validation cycle, including owners, gates, security
  boundaries, measures, and immediate decisions.
- [Company operating principles](PRINCIPLES.md): the ten principles governing
  product, customer, research, and company decisions.
- [Source register](SOURCES.md): public evidence, access dates, limitations, and
  links.

Chart images and machine-generated build inputs are intentionally omitted from
the repository. Their relevant values and assumptions remain in the editable
strategy, and the presentation charts remain embedded in the Word edition.

The archived October 1 [product business plan](../archive/BUSINESS_PLAN.md)
remains context for the earlier industrial offer. This package broadens company
planning and proposes an experiment. Company name, legal structure, funding,
spending, and the first commercial offer remain open decisions. Read the
recommendation as proposed until the founders record acceptance.

External sources support the problem landscape and competing capabilities. They do not establish customer willingness to pay or current company traction. Sensitive founder records, contacts, contracts, financial records and customer data should remain outside the repository.
